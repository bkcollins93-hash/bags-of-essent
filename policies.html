Policies & trust.

These policies explain how Bags of Essent serves its customers. This page is part of the BUC3106 E-Commerce project at MUBS. The website's checkout and payment features are demonstrations only.

Student project notice: This website is for BUC3106 at MUBS. It is a demonstration only; payments and real online ordering are not active. Do not submit personal or payment information here.

Delivery

Bags of Essent currently serves customers within Kampala. Delivery is available within 24 hours, depending on order arrangements. Delivery charges depend on the customer's location and distance. Customers may also collect their orders from the Bags of Essent shop in Bweyogerere, after Unique Hotel.

These delivery details are provided for the BUC3106 project. The website's checkout and payment features are demonstrations only and do not process real orders.

Returns, exchanges and refunds

Proposed policy for the Bags of Essent project: Customers should check their handbag upon delivery or collection. If a bag arrives damaged, the issue should be reported within 48 hours of delivery, with the order details and clear photographs where possible.

  • Damaged items: Report the damage within 48 hours. The item will be reviewed before an exchange or refund is considered.
  • Incorrect items: If the wrong bag, colour, size or other item is delivered, the proposed policy is to exchange it at no additional cost to the customer.
  • Refunds: Refunds are subject to inspection and approval. If the reported issue is confirmed, a full refund may be issued.
  • Customized items: Customized bags are not refundable unless they are damaged during delivery.

These are proposed terms for this student project. The website does not accept real orders or process refunds.

Privacy notice

Student project notice: Bags of Essent is a demonstration website for BUC3106 at MUBS. The checkout form is for demonstration purposes only. It does not send or store customer information, and no real orders or payments are processed. Please do not enter real personal or payment information.

Information shown in the demo: The checkout demonstration may request a customer's name, phone number, email address, delivery address, collection method and preferred payment method. These fields demonstrate how an online store may collect information needed to manage an order and arrange delivery.

Privacy if the business launches: A real Bags of Essent store should collect only information necessary for order management, customer communication and delivery. Customers should be told why their information is collected, who may access it, how it is protected and how long it is retained. Information should not be shared for unrelated purposes without a lawful basis.

Customer rights: Before a real launch, the business should establish a process for customers to ask about, correct or request deletion of their personal information where applicable. Its practices should be reviewed against Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019, and applicable guidance.

Terms of use

  • Business identity: Bags of Essent is the business featured in this website, which was developed as part of the BUC3106 E-Commerce course at MUBS.
  • Product information: Product names, descriptions, materials, availability and prices should be confirmed with the business before a purchase.
  • Orders and payments: The checkout and payment features on this coursework website are demonstrations only. No real orders, customer information or payments are processed through them.
  • Payment safety: Visitors should not enter payment credentials or send money using sample payment details displayed for the coursework.
  • Content and images: Website content and images must be original, owned by the business or group, or used with permission or an appropriate licence.
  • Acceptable use: Visitors must not misuse the website, attempt to access restricted areas, interfere with its operation or use its content to impersonate Bags of Essent.
  • Honest information: Product claims, prices and promotional statements must be truthful and supported by available evidence.

These terms explain the use of the BUC3106 coursework website. They should be reviewed and updated before the business begins accepting real online orders.

Honest product claims

Bags of Essent aims to provide accurate product descriptions and prices. Product names, brands, materials, availability and other details should be verified before being advertised. Claims such as genuine leather, waterproof, handmade or imported should only be used when they can be supported by reliable information. Customers should confirm product availability and details before making a purchase.
