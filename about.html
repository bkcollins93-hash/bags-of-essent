Bags of Essent

Everyday elegance, your way.

Bags of Essent is a growing handbag business established in 2026, offering stylish and practical handbag choices for women.

We recognise that handbags are more than fashion accessories. They are part of everyday life, helping women carry their essentials while expressing their individuality and confidence.

Our aim is to make it easier for women to find handbags suitable for different lifestyles, activities and occasions, whether going to work, attending school, travelling or enjoying a special occasion.

Our Mission

To offer stylish, practical and convenient handbag choices that help women express their personal style and confidence.

What We Stand For

  • Personal Style: Handbag choices that complement different preferences and occasions.
  • Practicality: Styles suited to everyday activities and different needs.
  • Convenience: Making it easy for customers to browse products and understand how to order.
  • Honest Service: Providing clear and reliable product, pricing and delivery information.
Our Promise

Style, functionality and convenience.

At Bags of Essent, we aim to make handbag shopping a simple and pleasant experience by combining style, functionality and customer convenience.
