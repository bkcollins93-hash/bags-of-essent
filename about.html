Our story

Exclusively for her.

A handbag brand concept centred on personal style, confidence and thoughtful details.

Bags of Essent

Everyday elegance, your way.

Bags of Essent is presented as a women's handbag business offering different styles to suit different tastes, routines and occasions. The aim of this student project is to make it easier for customers to discover products online, review product information and understand how to place an order.

This page is a draft brand introduction. The business owner should approve or edit it to reflect the real story and values of the business.

What we want to stand for

  • Personal style: a range of looks for different preferences.
  • Clear information: product names, prices and availability that customers can check.
  • Convenience: simple online browsing and order guidance.
  • Honest service: clear delivery details and no unsupported product claims.
Our promise

Beautiful choices. Clear details.

We aim to help every customer find a bag that feels right for her. Product materials, craftsmanship, warranty, stock and delivery timing will be stated only when confirmed by the business.
