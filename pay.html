How to order & pay.

Choose your favourite handbag, place your order and select how you would like to receive it.

How to place your order

  1. Browse our collection.
    Choose your handbag and add it to your shopping cart.
  2. Review your shopping cart.
    Check your selected products, quantities and total before checkout.
  3. Choose your payment and delivery option.
    Select shop pickup, payment on delivery or Mobile Money payment before delivery.
  4. Receive order confirmation.
    For prepaid orders, confirmation is sent through WhatsApp and email. A payment screenshot may be requested as proof.
