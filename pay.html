Simple steps

How to order & pay.

Use this guide to understand the proposed ordering process. This is a student demonstration website; payments are not active.

Place an order in three steps.

  1. Choose your bag.
    Note the product name or code and check the listed sample price.
  2. Confirm details.
    Contact the business to confirm the real price, stock, colour and delivery fee.
  3. Agree on delivery.
    The project delivery area is Kampala. Confirm the location and timing before proceeding.
